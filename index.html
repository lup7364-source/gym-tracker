How to weigh yourself

  1. Weigh in once a week, on the same day, first thing in the morning.
  2. Go to the bathroom first, then step on the scale right after.
  3. Empty stomach: no food, and don’t drink any water until you’ve weighed.
  4. Same scale, on a flat hard floor, with no clothes (or always the same).
  5. Weigh before any workout.
